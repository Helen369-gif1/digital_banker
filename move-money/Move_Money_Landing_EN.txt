MOVE MONEY
Landing page copy — English

SCREEN 1 — HERO

Move money. Stay in control.

Send, receive, and manage payments in one place. Whether you're paying someone, adding funds, or moving money between accounts, Move Money makes the next step clear — with Gia ready to help.

Primary button: Explore Move Money
Secondary button: Ask Gia


SCREEN 2 — EVERYDAY MONEY MOVEMENT

All the ways you move money.

Send & Pay
Send money to people or businesses, pay another Glonari member, or scan a QR code to pay.

Receive & Request
Share your payment details or QR code, request money, and follow each request until it's paid.

Add, Withdraw & Convert
Add money from a linked account, withdraw to an external account, or convert eligible Glonari assets. See the details before you proceed.


SCREEN 3 — MAKE REPEAT PAYMENTS EASIER

Less work for the things you do often.

Return to recent recipients, schedule a payment, set up recurring transfers, or split a shared expense with others. Find past transactions, check their status, and open a receipt whenever you need it.


SCREEN 4 — GIA HELPS WITH THE DETAILS

Just tell Gia what you need to do.

“Gia, send Curt $150.”

Gia can find the recipient, check available funds, prepare the transfer, and explain fees, timing, or restrictions before you decide. If a payment could affect funds reserved for another goal, she'll let you know.

You can also complete actions yourself. Gia is there when you want help, not in your way.


SCREEN 5 — YOU APPROVE

Prepared by Gia. Approved by you.

Before money moves, review the recipient, amount, funding source, fees, and timing. You choose whether to approve or decline. Gia never completes a protected financial action without your explicit approval.

Clear steps. Visible activity. Your decision.


SCREEN 6 — FINAL CTA

Your money, moving your way.

From quick payments to planned transfers, Move Money brings your everyday money actions together — with Gia helping at every step and you making the final call.

Button: Open Move Money
